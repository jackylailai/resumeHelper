Senior Backend Engineer

We are looking for a senior backend engineer to join our platform team. You will:

- Design and operate FastAPI services backed by PostgreSQL.
- Integrate LLM providers (Anthropic Claude preferred) with caching and
  observability.
- Drive test-driven development, owning unit, integration and contract test
  layers.

Required:
- 5+ years Python, including 2+ years FastAPI or comparable async framework.
- Strong PostgreSQL fundamentals, SQLAlchemy 2.x, Alembic.
- Production experience with prompt-driven LLM features.
- TDD discipline and comfort with pytest, schemathesis, or equivalent.

Nice to have:
- Docker / Kubernetes operational experience.
- Open-source contributions in observability or workflow orchestration.
